# Data Processing Agreement — Acme SaaS Inc.

**Effective:** 2026-01-15

## Clause 4 — Data residency

4.1 The Processor shall store and process Customer Personal Data within data
centers located in the **United States and the European Union only**.

4.2 The Processor shall **not transfer Customer Personal Data to any jurisdiction
outside those named in 4.1** without the Controller's prior written consent and
an approved transfer mechanism.

## Clause 5 — Subprocessors

5.1 The Processor maintains a current list of subprocessors and shall notify the
Controller of any intended additions or replacements at least 30 days in advance.

5.2 Each subprocessor is bound by data protection obligations no less protective
than those in this Agreement.

## Clause 7 — Security measures

7.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational
measures, including encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest, and
access controls enforcing least privilege.

## Clause 9 — Breach notification

9.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller **without undue delay and no later
than 72 hours** after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.
